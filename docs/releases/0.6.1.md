### Cleaner edges with custom camera backgrounds

Custom background images now suppress low-confidence background spill while
preserving soft foreground edges and premultiplied colors. Background blur and
transparent removal are unchanged.

A real Elgato Facecam 4K test under Wayland exposed distracting background residue
with custom images. The corrected live result was accepted as significantly better
and usable during head/hand movement. Background blur already passed that live test.
Private camera frames are not included in this repository or release.

The final Ubuntu 26.04 package was installed and verified on Ubuntu 26.04.1.
Native Wayland checks passed for blur, custom images, rounded corners, alpha
cleanup and saved-scene restoration with the real Facecam. The tested sessions
closed without warning/error diagnostics or reported memory leaks.

### Follow-up motion test — known limitation

A later real Facecam recording test found partially disappearing hands during
fast motion with a custom background. The head remained intact. The installed
build's 70-second recording decoded correctly and closed cleanly, but logged one
lagged render frame and one skipped encode frame. Experimental model/alpha
adjustments did not reliably solve the visual issue and were not shipped.
See [motion validation](../validation/facecam-motion-0.6.1.md).
