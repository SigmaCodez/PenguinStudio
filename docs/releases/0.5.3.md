### Camera motion and background removal

- Automatic background removal now computes the mask from the frame being rendered. Previously, applying the previous frame's mask could reveal background strips or clip moving hands and heads.
- A newly enabled filter now masks its first rendered frame, preventing a brief flash of the unfiltered background.
- Rounded corners and the existing RVM model, quality settings and CPU thread policy are unchanged.

Native Wayland tests covered real Facecam head and hand movements, a comparison
using the same recorded input, camera appearance controls, and clean shutdown.
The new first-frame regression fails on 0.5.2 and passes with the correction.
The corrected 20-second recordings fully decoded; each reported one render/encode
stall (about 0.2%), with no warnings, errors or reported memory leaks on shutdown.
Fast movement in a blurred camera image can still leave soft or incomplete edges;
this correction does not promise perfect segmentation or zero dropped frames.

Camera test footage remains private and is not included in the release.
